EMERGENCY

Call 911 now

Flames or a smoke column can mean a fire near you.

Tell the dispatcher

  1. 1Where you are: address or nearest road and town
  2. 2What you see: flames, a smoke column, or dark smoke
  3. 3Which way it’s moving, if you can tell
  4. 4If anyone is in danger or needs help
Call 911

Stay on the line and follow the dispatcher. If the fire is close to you, move away while you talk.
