How this works

Flames

Fire you can see outdoors, other than a campfire or fire pit someone is watching.

Smoke column

Smoke rising from one spot. Smoke from far away spreads out as haze; a column means the fire is close.

Dark smoke

Thick black or brown smoke. It often means a building, vehicle or dense brush is burning nearby.
