# Report: Feature 5, "Is burning allowed today?"

Overnight lane, Oct 4, 2026. Branch `feat/burn`, worktree `sof-burn`. Committed locally; nothing pushed, no other
branch touched. Decision record: [docs/decisions/0005-burn.md](../decisions/0005-burn.md).

## In one paragraph

An official machine-readable source exists, so the feature was built. Under "Why?" on the verdict screen, in New
Brunswick, one card says for the person's county whether burning is allowed today, from the province's own burn
category layers, or says "Not checked". It never changes the verdict. A fresh reviewer then tried to break it and
found 19 things; all were fixed or recorded as limits.

## Three things for you to look at first

1. **I departed from the brief's season rule.** The brief said "outside the fire season (after Oct 31, before the
   third Monday of April): closed if the source says so, else not checked". The province's own dashboard says 2026's
   season ran "from Wednesday April 1st to October 31st", and its season page says a season "may be extended". The
   brief's rule would have hidden a real "No burn" on April 5, 2026. So a category the province gives is shown
   whatever the date, and "Fire season closed" is said only when the province lists the county with no category from
   November to March.
2. **November 1 is the first real test.** Nobody can know today what the layers do when the season ends. The card
   will read "Fire season closed" if the counties move to "No Current Category", and "Not checked" if old categories
   are left in place. Both are true; a look on November 1 will tell which.
3. **Green is on this card, and nowhere else.** DESIGN-LOCK says no green. The brief asked for green. It is the
   outline and shape of "Burning permitted" only, with a flame inside and no check mark, so it cannot read as "all
   clear" under a verdict that says to look outside. DESIGN-LOCK has an amendment saying so.

## The independent review

A fresh agent, given the code and the requirements and told to break it, reported 19 findings. The table of what was
found and what was done is in the decision record. The ones that changed behaviour:

- the season rule (above);
- a screen left open kept an old answer: the card now follows the phone's clock;
- "for this evening, check again after 2 p.m.", because a status seen in the morning says nothing of tonight;
- six islets of the province's Charlotte County outline are in Maine: left out;
- "Not checked" cards appeared in Calais and Pointe-à-la-Croix: now hidden;
- "allows campfires and other outdoor wood fires" overclaimed: now "campfires and small wood fires. Bigger fires need
  a permit";
- a green tick under "unexplained smoke": now a flame.

It also confirmed what holds: the point-in-polygon code, the border with Maine, New Brunswick's islands, the reading
of `VALIDDATE`, and that each cited source says what is credited to it.

## A 20-second demo

The deployed engine does not have the field yet (nothing was pushed). To show it live from this checkout: in
`engine/`, `uv run uvicorn smoke_engine.main:app --port 8000`; in `web/`, `VITE_ENGINE_URL=http://localhost:8000 npm run dev`.

1. Open the app live, answer the three questions, pick **Moncton**. *(5 s)*
2. On the verdict, scroll past **Why?** to **Is burning allowed today?** Read aloud: "Westmorland County. Burning
   permitted. Valid until 2 p.m. today. For this evening, check again after 2 p.m." *(6 s)*
3. Point at the shape: "A flame in a green circle. No burning is a red octagon, restricted an amber triangle: never
   colour alone." *(4 s)*
4. Tap **Sources**: "The province's own data, by county. Our server asks for all 15 counties; your location never
   goes to the province." *(3 s)*
5. Switch to **Replay**: "Not checked. The province keeps no past status, so we do not guess." *(2 s)*
