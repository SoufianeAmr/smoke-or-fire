## Method

### The air, traced backward from you

- Starts at your point at the time of the check and steps back 1 hour at a time, for up to 24 steps (`smoke_engine/trajectory.py`).
- Integration: Heun predictor-corrector. Each step makes a first guess with the wind at the current point, then moves from the current point with the average of the wind there and at the guess. Positions move on a sphere of radius 6371 km.
- Heights: `100m`, `925hPa` and `850hPa`, each traced separately.
- Wind: Open-Meteo, weather model `gfs025`, hourly speed and direction on a 0.5° grid from 41°N to 51°N and 72°W to 56°W: 21 × 33 = 693 points. Between grid points and hours the wind is interpolated (bilinear in space, linear in time). A trace stops where its next point would leave the grid.
- Wind steadiness: the circular standard deviation of the hourly wind direction along the path; above 45° is unsteady.

### Fires near the path

- Fires within 500 km of you: CWFIS active fire records at stage `BH` and `OC` (being held, out of control; under control is left out), and satellite detections from the 24 hours before the check. Detections within 5 km of each other chain into one fire; a CWFIS record within 5 km joins that fire.
- Detections kept: VIIRS nominal and high confidence (low is dropped); MODIS confidence 30 or more on FIRMS's scale.
- One observation, counted once: a CWFIS hotspot within 50 m of a FIRMS detection with the same fire radiative power (within 0.05 MW) is that detection, republished; otherwise a CWFIS hotspot from the same satellite within 1 km and 30 minutes of a FIRMS detection merges into it. Detections from different satellites are separate observations.
- Closest approach: the shortest distance between the path's hourly segments and each fire's points. A fire counts only if its closest point on the path comes after the start: a fire whose closest point is your own position is reported as the nearest fire, not as the air's source.

### Verdict and confidence

The table in [design/DESIGN-LOCK.md](design/DESIGN-LOCK.md), as `smoke_engine/verdict.py` computes it:

| Closest approach | Wind steady | Wind unsteady |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 10 km | Drifting, High | Unclear, Low |
| over 10, up to 25 km | Drifting, Medium | Unclear, Low |
| over 25, up to 50 km | Unclear, Medium | Unclear, Low |
| over 50 km, or no fire | Unexplained, Medium | Unexplained, Low |

- The height whose path came closest to a fire gives the verdict. When the 3 heights do not all give the same verdict, confidence is one level lower (high → medium, medium → low, low → low).
- Confidence is only ever High, Medium or Low.
- No active fire within 500 km: unexplained smoke, with no fire shown (screen 7d).

### The fire's smoke, traced forward

- The fire: the closest approach's fire for drifting smoke and unclear, the nearest fire for unexplained smoke, none when no fire is in range.
- Smoke leaves the fire every hour over the 24 hours before the check, at each of the 3 heights: 72 paths, each followed forward with the same integrator and wind grid until the time of the check or the grid edge (`smoke_engine/forward.py`).
- `forward.closestKm` is the smallest distance from any path segment to you, with its release time and height. `forward.agrees` is true when it is 25 km or less. A path whose closest point to you is the fire itself (its smoke moved away from you) never makes it agree.
- Informational only: it is computed after the verdict and never changes the verdict or the confidence (`test_the_forward_trace_never_changes_the_verdict` in `engine/tests/test_verdict_api.py`). The answer carries the 24 paths of the closest height.
